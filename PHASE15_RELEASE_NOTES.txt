AgriORBIT Phase 15 — Immersive Orbital Boot (portfolio research prototype)

Features: supplied AgriORBIT emblem embedded in browser HUD; orbital calibration animations;
radar sweep, decorative particles, progress-readiness synchronization; 4 real startup checks;
automatic mission transition, replay and presentation hold mode; reduced-motion support.

Integrity: boot animation is not NASA data retrieval, rover calibration, validated field sensing,
or NASA approval. The previous historical NASA data, crop comparison and guidelines features remain.

Browser screenshot test: blocked by Chrome policy in the current environment; user should verify visually on their Windows machine.
