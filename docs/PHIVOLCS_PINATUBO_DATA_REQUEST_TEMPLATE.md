**Subject:** Request for authorized Pinatubo lahar/flood hazard GIS layer for agricultural-resilience research

Dear PHIVOLCS Data Services Team,

Our research/portfolio project, AgriORBIT NASA (Sammium AgriMind AI), is developing a farmer-facing decision-support prototype for a permitted agricultural study area in Botolan, Zambales, Philippines. We seek guidance on obtaining an appropriately licensed, current **Pinatubo lahar and related flooding hazard** GIS layer that may be used for **private local research and scientific visualization**, and on whether any public web-map presentation would require separate permission.

Please advise us on:

1. The most recent authoritative Pinatubo lahar/flood hazard map edition and legend relevant to Botolan, Zambales;
2. Available formats (GeoPackage, SHP, GeoJSON or service access), CRS, scale, version and publication date;
3. The application process, required Data User Agreement / Memorandum of Agreement, cost if any, attribution and permitted uses;
4. Whether local polygon intersection analysis, derived maps or screenshots can be shared publicly as part of a research demo;
5. Any necessary caveats for hazards that change over time and whether a parcel-specific official Hazard Assessment Report is recommended.

The project will not describe historical NASA rainfall or user-computed GIS overlap as a real-time lahar forecast, official safety clearance, or a substitute for PHIVOLCS assessment. We will honor source restrictions and will not redistribute agency vectors unless specifically authorized.
