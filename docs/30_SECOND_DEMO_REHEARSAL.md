# Demo rehearsal — not yet submitted

**This script and outline are editorial aids, not NASA-prescribed text.** The uploaded 2026 Judging & Awards Guide (p. 3) permits either a video at most **30 seconds** or slides at most **7 total** for the short Global Judging demo.

## Suggested 30-second voiceover

“Farmers in Botolan face changing rainfall and uncertain planting choices. AgriORBIT brings NASA POWER historical climate context together with farmer-entered soil information and priorities. We compare three experimental crop rotations and show the data behind the trade-offs. Our rover and holographic field are simulations, while agronomic recommendations still require local validation. Our aim is to help farmers discuss climate-adaptive options with transparent evidence.”

Practice aloud and shorten if it runs over thirty seconds. Rehearse with a real date-stamped NASA observation **only if actually retrieved**. Otherwise say "the data interface is under test" and clearly mark synthetic demonstrations.

## Seven-slide alternative (maximum; no extra title slide)

1. Title, problem and team.
2. Farmer need and permitted Botolan pilot context.
3. NASA POWER dataset, actual variables, year range, units, coordinates and provenance.
4. Local soil and farmer priority inputs.
5. Baseline, legume and diversified rotation comparison.
6. Transparent heuristic scoring, source limitations and simulated rover label.
7. Farmer value, validation roadmap, references and acknowledgments.

## Narration & accessibility

- English-language project page and linked materials. Non-English audio in a video **must** have English subtitles (Guide p. 3).
- Never imply measured rover data when it is synthetic.
- Include source and rights acknowledgments for external material.
- Confirm local event's independent presentation rules, if any.
